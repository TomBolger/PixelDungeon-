      How this works

      These are full runs of a bot playing a faithful port of Pixel Dungeon 1.x made for the Pebble smartwatch. It plays the real game: no challenges, no head start, no extra lives, and it only knows what the hero could know. It hasn't won a single game yet. Skilled players say they win most of their runs, so the bot is missing something, and that's what we'd like your help with.

      Reading the screen

      • The map shows what the hero can see now (bright) and remembers (dim). The orange reticle is where the bot is heading this step; blue letters are the other options it weighed.
      • From here, the bot decided is the bot's own reason for the move, such as walk to 12,5 [explore]. Coordinates are column,row on the map.
      • What happened is the game log for that step. A red flash means the hero took damage.
      • Hover or tap any backpack slot to see the item's description.
      • Tick Show what the bot can't see to reveal the whole floor, including hidden doors and traps (pink).

      Giving feedback

      Pause on a moment that looks wrong, press Suggest a better move, and say what you'd do. The note is saved with the exact game and step, so it can be replayed. Notes about overall strategy are just as welcome (choose "the whole game's strategy"). Your name is optional.

      Sharing a moment

      Copy link gives a link that opens this exact step of this game.

      Privacy

      A note stores what you type, the moment it's about and the time. To block spam, it also stores a scrambled code made from your connection, which can't be turned back into your address. Nothing else is kept, and there are no ads or trackers.
