# Pixel Dungeon Face (watchface)

A watchface that plays Pixel Dungeon by itself. Same game code as `watch/` (nothing is forked); the face is a compile switch (`PD_FACE`).

- Build: `face/build.sh` (copies `watch/` to `face/work/`, sets the face's UUID and `PD_FACE`, runs `watch/tools/build.sh`). Output `face/pixel-dungeon-face.pbw`. Needs `PD_ROOT` pointing at a watabou/pixel-dungeon checkout when `third_party/pixel-dungeon` is empty.
- Test builds: `FACE_DEFINES=FACE_WINTEST` (starts holding the Amulet: shows the victory screen), `FACE_NEWGAME` (ignores the save), `FACE_LOGP` (logs every press).
- The face's own overlay `face/ovl/fstage` (the starting stage's setup) is copied into `face/work/src/ovl` and registered as a last resource (`OVL_FSTAGE`) by `build.sh`; the normal app has neither (`bot/build.sh -DPD_FACE` builds it for the host tests too).
- Code: `watch/src/c/face.inc` (clock, bursts, driving the windows), `face_brain.h` (rules on top of the Assist's target list, shared with the host test), cheats in `game.c` under `PD_FACE`.
- Host test: `cd bot; OUT=facetest MAIN=tests/facetest.c ./build.sh -DPD_FACE; D=1 build/face/facetest 12` (with `-DPD_FACE` the overlays are built with the switch too, in `bot/build/face/`; `VARY=1` gives every game its own dungeon, else the games repeat per class; it prints the share of game time resting, healing drinks, starving, the HP spread).

## Settings (round 2)
- The face has a Clay settings page (phone side: `face/pkjs/`; `build.sh` fetches the pebble-clay script; the normal app has no JS). The phone sends the settings as one 13-byte array (`CFG`, same order as `FaceCfg` in face.inc; byte 4 is a reserved 0, once "clock inside the HUD"; an old 12-byte save still loads, its old HUD clock becoming the time and date slots) and the weather as three int16 tenths of a degree C (`WX`); the watch keeps both in persist storage.
- The big clock sits between the HUD and the hero (windows get a small clock at the top). Each HUD number (floor, keys, level, health, and the backpack's gold) is a slot: the game's number, or time, date, weather (Open-Meteo, phone location, refreshed every 45 min), steps, heart rate, battery, day, and more. The level frame widens to the left by itself whenever the level slot shows anything but the game's number. "Date order" (settings, with the slots) is used by every Date slot. "Vibrate for game events" (default off) turns on the buzz when the hero is hurt or dies (the normal app always buzzes).
- The button tags and menu button on the right edge are not drawn in the face.
- Test builds: `FACE_FAKE` (steps 8412, heart rate 72, weather 21.5 C), `FACE_BAGTEST` (starts with the backpack open), `FACE_LOGS` (log lines and the lowest free heap; the shipped face has no log lines, to save RAM).

Round 2 (bot behaviour, toolbar, memory):
- Toolbar: the "?" button is a third quickslot (`Hero.quick[3]` in the face; face saves have their own magic). Wands and thrown weapons are used from their slot (press it, then aim); only consumables go through the backpack. The bot keeps its best wands/missiles in the slots (`fq_plan` in face.inc), re-assigning through item window > Quickslot > slot.
- Camera: Assist High (targets within the middle zoom's view, as in round 1) with the closest zoom as the home zoom. `face_cam_ok` (face.inc) gates main.c's `cam_to`: close in for a fight; a new far target zooms out to show both, holds ~1.1 s, then closes in for the run; no change within 1.2 s of the last.
- Amulet pickup with a full pack: `FB_DROP` in face_brain.h drops the least useful item first.
- Sound is stubbed out in the face (sfx.c); a new game frees the old floor's pictures first. Each pause logs the lowest free heap and the overlay loading at the time.
- Test builds (round 3): `FACE_NEWGAME FACE_QPTEST` (javelins, tomahawks etc. in the pack: watch the quickslot plan), `FACE_CAMLOG` (with FACE_HEAPLOG: a few log lines).
- Host: `bot/tests/levtest.c` (traps room + chest + Levitation).

## Pacing and wake-ups (owner round 4)
- Measured with `FACE_ACTLOG` (one log line per game turn; `FACE_OLDPACE` restores the old timings to compare). Causes found: the far-target camera glance held 1.1 s plus a 450 ms wait after it (about 2 s of no play each, every few seconds); an idle tick waited up to a second past the bot's next decision (`face_wake` in face.inc now cuts the wait to it); STEP_MS 140 and NAV_MS 330 (window presses) are now 100 and 200.
- The glance is now 650 ms (the swoop out and a moment to read), at most every 3 s; the camera closes in while the walk starts (main.c `act` centres it), no wait.
- The emulator's clock steps back about a second at times, which stalls a wait set from a late read: `face_tick` drops any wait over 700 ms x the Game speed's decision multiplier (no real one is longer; day-WL).
- Back and the other buttons: nothing reaches a watchface (a click handler never fires). Emulator: on Emery a Back press produces no event at all; on Gabbro it closes the face (window `disappear`, the app exits) and the system launches it again, which starts a burst (`face_boot`); Up/Down/Select close it the same way and Back brings it back (a fresh launch, a burst). So a quick Back cannot be made to start play; shake still does. `FACE_BTNTEST` logs these (`EV` codes in face.inc). The timeline's quick view fires the unobstructed-area `did_change`, and an app-focus regain `did_focus`: both start a burst.

## A livelier face (round 5: less resting, fewer pinned fights)
All of this is `PD_FACE` only (the watch app's code and overlays are unchanged); the numbers are constants in `game_int.h`.
- No more forever food: food is found and eaten as in the game. Instead the hero starts with one identified Potion of Healing that is never used up (`game.c` keeps one in the pack after every turn: drunk, thrown, dropped, stolen, it is back; it cannot be sold, `game_pick_ok`). The Assist drinks it in a fight under a quarter health (as before) and in place of a rest when under 40% (`tgt.c`, before the rest rule); `FACE_HEAL_CD` (40 game turns) is the pause between two drinks. Only the healing colour is known; the other potions are not identified.
- Hunger runs `FACE_HUNGER_X` (8) times slower, the stairs cost 1/8 of their hunger too: without the long rests the hero plays about five times faster than the food in the dungeon allows (at x1 he starved most of the game).
- Gear never breaks: durability can fall (and scrolls and enchanting still repair it) but stops at 1 (`item_use`).
- Better gear, a little: weapons and armour get a level more often (45% instead of 40%) and upwards more often (60% instead of 50% of those: +1..+3 from 20% to 27% of items, cursed from 20% to 18%); armour tiers 4:4:5:5:5 instead of even, melee weapon tiers 1: 3, 2-3: 4, 4-5: 5 where each had 4. The Assist still never reads unknown levels.
- Item screens dwell about half a second: `NAV_MS` 480 (was 260), the item-window select wait 500 (was 400), the backpack-open wait stays 650 (`face_tick` drops any wait over 700 ms). `FP_MS` (a plan's time limit) went from 12 to 18 s to match the slower presses.
- Passive regeneration is `FACE_REGEN_X` (3) times faster. This is tunable: if the HP bar sits pinned at full and the fights lose their tension, lower it (or raise `FACE_HEAL_CD`); if it sits near empty, do the opposite.

## Stuck safeguard (temporary, entertainment only)
`face_brain.h` `fb_stuck` (day-WI): when the hero has stayed within 2 cells of one spot for `FACE_STUCK_TURNS` (60) game turns, with no foe in sight and no health gained since, he is teleported at random as a Scroll of Teleportation would (no scroll used, nothing logged; a boss floor, where there is no room to do it, gets nothing). Hurt and resting counts a third as fast (3x the limit); a rest or search at full health counts in full. Host: `facetest` prints how often it fired (`VARY=1 build/face/facetest 40`: 14 times in 40 games / 2098 floors; `TELEDBG=1` lists them). It hides stuck spots instead of fixing them: remove it once the stuck causes are fixed.

A second, broader rule (day-WJ, `fb_noprog`, `FACE_NOPROG_TURNS` = 150): none of a cell seen for the first time, something picked up (gold or anything in the pack grew), the floor changed, a creature killed (`g_face_kills`, counted in game.c `mob_kill`), health gained while below the maximum, for 150 game turns, and he is teleported the same way. Turns with a foe in sight do not count (the count restarts). It catches pacing about a larger area, which the 2-cell rule never saw. Host numbers (`VARY=1 build/face/facetest 40`, 2086 floors): the small-area rule 12 times, the no-progress rule 46 times, but on only about 20 floors (a floor he cannot get off, e.g. 6 times on one floor with the Amulet, fires every 150 turns until it works); a Magic Mapped floor (all cells "seen" at once) followed by a very long walk is the one false case seen.

## Traps (day-WI)
The Assist (ovl/tgt, tgt4 part) now handles known traps: a traps room's prize is looted from the doorway by throwing spare things onto the traps along the way (farthest first; a thing landing on a trap springs it, Level.drop), backing off from the gas, waiting it out and walking in over the dead traps; the Levitation potion is kept. Shut in by known traps with no trap-free way to the stairs or the unexplored: the same throws (the face only: the buttons cannot aim at a cell; `face.inc` aims a thrown thing at the Assist's entry cell when no enemy is in view), else the cheapest way over the traps, resting first when the next trap could kill. Host: `bot/tests/levtest.c` (`OUT=levtest MAIN=tests/levtest.c ./build.sh [-DPD_FACE]`).

## Slimming (day-WJ)
Room for the coming settings feature. Face only (`PD_FACE`; the normal app and its overlays are unchanged):
- Overlays the face never opens are emptied or cut down in the source (`#ifndef PD_FACE`): `sheet` (character sheet, item catalog, journal) is an empty entry; `ui` keeps only the two endings (no title, hero select, rankings, erase confirm); `uiw` keeps the quest/option window and the game-over banner (no game menu, no info window); `info` keeps the item window's text (no cell/monster inspect, no terrain names); `snd` is empty (the face is silent) and `face/build.sh` replaces the music and sound data (125 KB) with one byte each; `mobs` no longer keeps the journal records every turn. Every app function that only these overlays called is no longer kept by the linker (`keep_syms.txt` fell from 322 to about 300).
- `pd.h`: in the face `AS_ON/AS_ITEMS/AS_FAR/AS_OFFCAM` are constants (always Assist High, set in `face_boot`), so the other modes' code in main.c is not built; the tgt overlays keep the variable (`PD_AS_DYN`: their size is tuned to the arena).
- main.c: no message windows (signs, story chapters, a creature's plain words: `ui_message` does nothing and the window code for them is out); the saved zoom/Assist is not read; the text page tap and the "out of memory for this window" text are out.
- Result: app footprint gabbro 65531 -> 64185 B (free heap 65541 -> 66887), emery 64957 -> 63391 B (free heap 66115 -> 67681); package resources 567 KB -> 431 KB.

## Settings round 6: Controls, Cheats, starting stage, classes (day-WK)
The Clay page (`pkjs/config.js`, packed by `pkjs/index.js` into the 21-byte `CFG`, same order as `FaceCfg`; the watch still loads the old 12- and 13-byte saves, the new bytes then keep their defaults; the inbox is 32 bytes now) has four new sections at the top. Everything is `PD_FACE` only; the normal app's code and overlays are byte-for-byte what they were (`watch/tools/build.sh`: all overlays "(unchanged)", Free RAM 65565 emery / 65744 gabbro).
- **Controls**: two switches, "Start a new run" and "Teleport the hero". When the page is saved with one on, `pkjs` sends the settings, then (after they landed) one `CMD` message (1 new run, 2 teleport; both on: the new run) and sets both switches back to off in Clay's stored settings, so they are off the next time the page opens. The watch keeps the command (`s_f_cmd`), starts a burst, and carries it out at the next think (`face_cmd`): a new run is `face_new_game()` (windows closed first; it uses the class boxes and the starting stage); the teleport is `fb_teleport()` (face_brain.h, the very code the stuck rules use), waiting until no window is open; on a boss floor there is no room for it and nothing happens.
- **Cheats** (with the page's sentence under the heading), `FaceRules g_fr` (game_int.h, defined in game.c; `face_rules_apply` copies the settings into it; the host tests set it):
  - *God mode* (default off): `hero_damage` never lets health fall below 1 (`hero_die` has no other caller: starving, falls, traps, explosions, gas and the rest all come through it). NOTE: before this setting the face clamped health at 1 unconditionally, so "off" is a change from the old behaviour; the page default follows the request (off). The low-health dodging (`face_evades`) stays always.
  - *Healing speed* (default 3x): Normal / 2x / 3x / 5x replaces `FACE_REGEN_X`.
  - *Ever-present healing potion* (default on): off = no starting potion, none kept in the pack after each turn, and the potion can be sold; the Assist's drink-instead-of-rest then only uses potions he found (the healing colour is not known at the start either).
  - *Unbreakable gear* (default on), *Better loot* (default on; off = the original odds in `ovl/gen`, same random draws as the normal app), *Slow hunger* (default on: `FACE_HUNGER_X` 8; off: 1).
  - With the defaults and god mode on the games are the ones of round 5 exactly (`D=1 build/face/facetest 12`: same 12 games, 12/12).
- **Starting stage** (default Sewers): Sewers 1, Goo 5, Prison 6, Tengu 10, Caves 11, DM-300 15, Dwarven City 16, Dwarf King 20, Demon Halls 21, Yog-Dzewa 25. The new game overlay (`ovl/newgame`, PD_FACE) loads `fstage` before the first floor is built, so the floor is generated directly at that depth (`FO_BUILD`), `max_depth` is that depth, and the overlay is gone again before the floor is built (the new-game heap peak is that of a floor 1 start). `fstage` gives the hero: a level (6, 7, 11, 12, 15, 16, 19, 20, 22), the strength potions and upgrade scrolls a player would have found above (the generator's quota counters `pos_count`, `sou_count`, `soe_count` are set to match, so the floors below keep dropping the rest), STR to match, a subclass from floor 11 (the Tome of Mastery comes from Tengu), gold, armour and weapon one or two tiers above a player's (tier 3 to 5, as far as his strength allows, the armour taking most of the upgrade scrolls: +2 at Goo to +8 at Halls/Yog; all known, none cursed), the mage's wand upgraded, and a backpack: seed pouch (scroll holder from Tengu, wand holster with a wand), a wand 55%, 3-8 potions, 3-8 scrolls, 2-4 seeds, food, a throwing stack 55%, a known sound ring 40%, a dew vial 60%; three backpack places are always left free. Known kinds (G.known) are drawn by the generator's own odds: 3 potion and 3 scroll kinds at Goo/Prison up to 9 and 9 at Yog (plus strength, upgrade, enchantment, healing). Nothing is told to the bot that the hero does not know (unknown potions and scrolls stay unknown).
- **Classes**: four boxes (a Clay checkbox group), all on by default; a new run picks one of the ticked at random, never the last run's unless only one is ticked, none ticked = all four (`fb_pick_class` in face_brain.h; with all four ticked it is the choice the face always made).
- Host: `bot/tests/facetest.c` takes `GOD` (default 1 there), `STAGE`, `CLS` (mask), `FR_REGEN/FR_HUNGER/FR_HEAL/FR_UNBREAK/FR_LOOT`, `START_INFO=1`; `sh bot/tests/settest.sh [games]` runs the whole check (defaults 12/12, every stage, god vs not, the class boxes, each cheat off).
- Sizes: app footprint gabbro 64677 -> 65261 B (Free RAM 66395 -> 65811; the cap is 65535), emery 63887 -> 64415 (67185 -> 66657).

## Settings round 7: Game speed and Battery saver (day-WL)
Two new sections on the page after Cheats-and-the-rest (before Vibration); `CFG` is 24 bytes now (`speed, burst, fps` appended to `FaceCfg`; the 12-, 13- and 21-byte saves still load, the new bytes keep their defaults = today's behaviour exactly). Face only; the normal app is unchanged (`watch/tools/build.sh`: Free RAM 65565 emery / 65744 gabbro, all overlay images identical).
- **Game speed**: Fast (default, the pacing as it was), Normal, Relaxed. `face_pace.h` (pure, no game headers; `bot/tests/pacetest.c`): decisions between moves x1 / 1.6 / 2.5 (`STEP_MS` 100 / 162 / 250, the camera glance hold 650 / 1056 / 1625, the 700 ms wait cap 700 / 1137 / 1750, the plan timeout `FP_MS` 18 / 29 / 45 s), a press in a window and the backpack / item waits x1 / 1.375 / 1.75 (`NAV_MS` 480 / 660 / 840, item select 500 / 687 / 875, bag open 650 / 893 / 1137). The cap and the plan timeout scale with the speed, so a slow mode neither cuts a wait short nor drops an item plan (a plan always has room for 37 presses, as before). The sprites' walk (133 ms a tile) is the game's own animation and is not scaled; Relaxed slows the decisions, fights and windows.
- **Play per wake** (Battery saver): Long 20 s (default), Medium 10 s, Short 5 s: `FACE_BURST_MS >> burst` in `face_burst`.
- **Frame rate**: Full (default; a 33 ms tick), Reduced (66 ms), Low (133 ms, and no far-target zoom-out glances: `face_cam` treats every target as one the close zoom shows). `busy()` in main.c schedules `face_fi()` (33 / 66 / 133 ms) and `face_wake` never waits less than that, so redraws and animation steps are that many per second at most; the bot's decisions still come at their own time (`s_f_next`). The sprites slide by elapsed time, so a walk keeps its speed: a tile takes 132 / 132 / 133 ms at the three rates (pacetest).
- Space: the new code cost 204 B (gabbro footprint 65261 -> 65465, 70 B under the cap; emery 64415 -> 64583). Paid for partly by `face_cfg_fix` no longer masking the settings the phone can only send as 0/1 (or that are masked where used).
- Check: `sh bot/tests/settest.sh` (the last check is `pacetest`); emulator, Emery, a temporary `FACE_ACTLOG` build (game turns played per wake; gabbro cannot fit the log build), settings sent through the real `pkjs` (`emu-app-config`): burst length exactly 20 / 10 / 5 s; turns per 20 s burst, 7-11 bursts each: Fast 58-122 (about 77), Normal 36-101 (about 64), Relaxed 20-59 (about 45); Short 5 s: 12-19 turns, Medium 10 s: 35-55; Reduced / Low frame rate: 60-95 / 56-76 turns per 20 s (no stalls, no dropped plans). The counts vary with what the hero meets (fights and windows are what the speed slows; walking is not).
